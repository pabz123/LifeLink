        Privacy

        Donors share a plan only if they tick the box in the app. You see a random code, the planned day and a status. You never see a name, phone number or blood group.

        Accounts are linked to one facility by an administrator. You can only see and change that facility's plans and requests.

        How a visit works

        1. A donor plans a donation in the app and ticks "Tell the hospital I am coming".
        2. The plan appears here, under Planned donations, and on the day it shows as Due today.
        3. When the donor arrives, ask for the code, open Confirm by code and confirm the donation.
        4. If the donor does not come, mark Did not attend. Plans whose day passed stay in Follow-up until you decide.

        If something looks wrong

        1. A code is not found: check the letters (the app avoids 0, O, 1 and I), and that the donor chose this facility.
        2. "The server refused this": your account may not be linked to this facility. Ask an administrator.
        3. A request does not reach a phone: phones check about every 15 minutes in the background, or at once when the app is open.
